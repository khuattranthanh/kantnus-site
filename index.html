Kantnus Publisher (our internal tool)

Kantnus Publisher is a private, internal application used only by the Kantnus Studio team to publish our own videos to our own channels on YouTube, TikTok and Facebook. It is not offered to the public and does not access any account other than the studio's own accounts.

  • Uploads finished videos, thumbnails and caption files to our own channels, and schedules their publication.
  • Updates the titles and thumbnails of our own videos (for title/thumbnail testing).
  • Reads aggregated performance statistics of our own channels to plan future videos.

How the tool handles data is described in our Privacy Policy.
