Data Deletion Instructions

Kantnus Publisher only stores data about the Kantnus Studio's own accounts (access tokens and statistics of our own videos). It does not store data about other Facebook, TikTok or YouTube users.

Remove the App's access yourself

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy › Settings › Business integrations (or Apps and websites) › select Kantnus Publisher › Remove.
  • TikTok: Profile › Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions › Kantnus Publisher › Remove access.
  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions › Kantnus Publisher › Remove access.

Once access is removed, the App can no longer read or post anything, and its stored tokens stop working.

Ask us to delete stored data

Email CONTACT_EMAIL with the subject “Data deletion request” and the account or page concerned. We delete the related tokens and statistics from our computer within 30 days and confirm by email.
