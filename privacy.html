Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Applies to: Kantnus Publisher (the “App”), operated by Kantnus Studio (“we”, “us”).

1. Who uses the App

The App is a private, internal tool. It is used only by members of the Kantnus Studio team to manage the studio's own channels and pages. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not access, collect or process data of any other user, creator or channel.

2. Data the App accesses

PlatformData accessed (studio's own accounts only)Why
YouTube / Google (YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API) Basic channel information (channel ID, title); our own videos' metadata (title, description, tags, privacy status, publish time, thumbnail, captions); aggregated statistics of our own videos (views, watch time, impressions, click-through rate). Upload and schedule our videos, upload captions and thumbnails, run title/thumbnail tests, plan future videos.
TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API) Basic profile of our own TikTok account (open ID, display name, avatar); creator settings needed to post (privacy options, maximum video length); status of the videos we post. Post our own short videos to our own TikTok account.
Meta (Facebook Pages API) The list of Facebook Pages we administer, a Page access token, and the status and basic insights of Reels we post on our own Pages. Publish and schedule our own Reels on our own Pages.

The App does not access comments or messages of other people, contacts, location, payment information, or any personal data of viewers.

3. How data is used

  • Only to publish and manage the studio's own content as described above.
  • Never sold, rented, shared with advertisers or data brokers, or used for advertising or profiling.
  • Never used to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • Never transferred to third parties, except where required by law.

Kantnus Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Storage and security

  • Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored only on the studio's own computer, in a local folder that is not synced to any cloud service and is excluded from source control. They are never displayed, logged or sent to anyone.
  • Statistics are stored in a local database on the same computer and used only for the studio's own reports.
  • Communication with each platform uses the platform's official API over encrypted HTTPS connections.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Statistics are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Tokens are deleted when access is revoked, when a channel stops being managed by the App, or on request.
  • You can revoke the App's access at any time: Google/YouTube — myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok — Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions; Facebook — Settings › Business integrations.
  • Deletion requests: see Data Deletion. We act on requests within 30 days.

6. Platform terms

By using features of the App that rely on YouTube, the user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok and Meta process data under their own policies: TikTok Privacy Policy, Meta Privacy Policy.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children and is used only by adult members of the studio team.

8. Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when the App's data practices change.
